Hongchao Qin （秦宏超）

Assistant Professor, School of Computer Science, Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT)

北京理工大学计算机学院个人主页 · BIT School of Computer Science profile

Education B.S., M.S., Ph.D., Northeastern University (2013, 2015, 2020)
Experience Postdoc at BIT (2020-2022); Research Assistant at SMU (2016-2017)

My earlier research focused on scalable data management and graph mining, including community search, temporal and dynamic graph analysis, higher-order graph structures, personalized search, and graph representation learning.

My current research focuses on memory-augmented self-evolving agents for long-horizon tasks: how to build data models that can iteratively update and evolve through experience. Besides, I also study on how to design the most efficient vector retrieval algorithms.

My research has resulted in 60+ peer-reviewed publications, including work in CCF-A venues such as SIGMOD, VLDB, ICDE, and ICML, as well as leading journals including IEEE TKDE.
